Frequently asked

Questions, answered

The things people asked us most last year. If your question isn't here, drop us a line via the contact page.

What is included in my ticket?

Access to the Blue and Black Slope sessions or Training Bar (workshop track). This depends on your ticket type. Food: lightweight breakfast, an extensive lunch, snacks during the breaks and the networking session. Drinks: coffee, tea and soda during the breaks + alcoholic beverages during the networking session.

Can I access the ski slope?

Access to the ski slope is not included in the Winterfest ticket, neither is skiing or snowboarding equipment. The ski slope is open for everyone, it is not privatized for Winterfest, following the regular pricing and conditions of SnowWorld Antwerpen.

Are the rooms accessible to persons with reduced mobility?

Yes, we are happy to provide assistance. Please leave us a message at team@fabricbelgium.be so we can facilitate all your needs.

Will the slide decks be distributed after the event?

We will distribute the slideware after the event, in accordance with our beloved speakers. The sessions will not be recorded.

What is the schedule?

We and our partners welcome you at 08:00 for a lightweight breakfast and the first coffee of the day. Lunch will be between 12:00 and 13:00. During the day we foresee two coffee breaks. At 17:00 we finish the day with a drink and by drawing a winner for the raffle. The speaker schedule will be published in the first half of November.

Where exactly is SnowWorld Antwerpen?

SnowWorld Antwerpen is located at Moerelei 123, 2610 Antwerpen. There's free parking on-site, and the venue is easily reached by car from Antwerpen-Zuid (≈10 minutes) or by public transport (Tram 5 has a stop nearby). See the Practicals section for a map and directions.

When do tickets go on sale?

Tickets will go on sale via Eventix in the second half of 2026. The exact date will be announced via Fabric Belgium's LinkedIn and on this site. Drop your email and we'll let you know the moment they open. Winterfest sells out every year.
