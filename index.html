What is Winterfest

A day on the knowledge slopes for data enthusiasts

Winterfest is the Belgian Fabric community's annual gathering: training, deep-technical sessions, real customer cases, and the warmest networking session of the year, at an indoor ski hall.

Real Belgian cases

Belgian partners and customers walking through what they actually built: wins, mistakes, regrets, and what they'd do differently with Fabric in 2026.

Hands-on training

A full-day Training Bar track for people new to Fabric: storage, ingestion, transformations, Power BI, DirectLake. The works, in one focused day.

Community & technical sessions

Coffee in the morning, an extensive lunch, two breaks, and a proper networking session with drinks at 17:00. The raffle at the end is part of the tradition.
