Code of Conduct

At Fabric Belgium, we are committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive, and ethical community.

All members, partners, speakers and attendees are expected to embody the following principles, at Winterfest and beyond.

1. Respect

Treat all individuals with dignity and consideration. Harassment, discrimination, or offensive behavior will not be tolerated.

2. Inclusivity

We value diversity and welcome people from all backgrounds. Support and encourage positive participation from everyone.

3. Integrity

Communicate honestly and constructively. Take responsibility for your actions and uphold the trust of the community.

4. Collaboration

Work together towards shared goals. Offer help when you can, and ask for it when you need it.

5. Compliance

Follow all applicable laws and regulations, and adhere to the guidelines set by Fabric Belgium.

Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in removal from our community. Together, we can create a positive, productive, and safe space for all.

If you witness or experience behavior that violates this Code, please contact the organizers at team@fabricbelgium.be. We take every report seriously.
